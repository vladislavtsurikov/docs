</Info>

This guide walks you through setting up a texture pass using the **Texture Stamper** tool and the **Unity/Terrain Texture** resource type. By the end you will have a working stamper that paints terrain textures driven by mask filters.

## Before you begin

You need:

- A Unity project with MegaWorld installed.
- A Unity Terrain in your scene.
- TerrainLayer assets ready in your Project window. You can create them via `Create > TerrainLayer`.

---

## Step 1 — Create a Texture Stamper

Go to `GameObject > Vladislav Tsurikov > Add Texture Stamper`.

A new **Texture Stamper** object appears in your Hierarchy. Its Transform scale defaults to `(150, 150, 150)`. Scale it to cover the area you want to paint.

---

## Step 2 — Add a Group

Select the Texture Stamper in the Hierarchy. In the **MegaWorld Window**, right-click in the Groups panel and choose **Create Group**.

Select **Unity/Terrain Texture** as the resource type for the Group.

---

## Step 3 — Add Terrain Texture prototypes

Inside the Group, click **Add Resource**. Drag your **TerrainLayer** assets from the Project window into the prototype list. Each TerrainLayer becomes its own prototype with independent filter settings.

---

## Step 4 — Configure Mask Filters

Select a prototype and expand its **Mask Filters** settings. Mask Filters determine where and how strongly each texture is painted. Common setups:

- **Slope filter** — paint rock textures on steep slopes and grass on flat ground.
- **Height filter** — snow at high elevations, mud at low elevations.
- **Noise filter** — add organic variation to texture boundaries.

If you do not add any filters, the texture is painted uniformly across the full stamper area at full strength.

---

## Step 5 — Set the area

The stamper area is controlled by the Transform scale of the Texture Stamper GameObject. Scale it in the Scene View to cover the terrain region you want to paint. Click **Fit To Terrain Size** to instantly expand the area to cover all terrains in the scene.

---

## Step 6 — Stamp

Click **Stamp**. The Texture Stamper finds all Unity Terrain tiles that intersect the stamper bounds and evaluates each prototype's Mask Filters, writing the results directly to the terrain alphamap.

To preview filter coverage before stamping, enable **Visualisation** in the Stamper Tool Controller. The Scene View shows green (will paint) and red (will not paint) areas for the selected Group.

---

## Step 7 — (Optional) Enable Auto Respawn

Turn on **Auto Respawn** in the Stamper Tool Controller to automatically re-stamp every time you change a filter setting. Use **Delay Auto Respawn** to add a short wait before each re-stamp triggers so it does not run on every keystroke.

<Tip>
  Work with a small area while tuning settings — a smaller area re-stamps much faster. Once the result looks right, click **Fit To Terrain Size** for the final pass.
</Tip>

---

## What's next

- Add multiple Groups to layer different textures across the same terrain area.
- Use [Grass Stamper](/megaworld/getting-started/grass-stamper) to fill the terrain with grass on top of the painted texture layers.
- Use [Texture Brush](/megaworld/getting-started/texture-brush) to hand-paint textures in specific spots.
- Read the full [Texture Stamper](/megaworld/tools/texture-stamper) reference for all settings.
