---
title: "MegaWorld"
description: "A tool ecosystem for procedural generation, semi-procedural workflows, and manual level design"
---

MegaWorld is an ecosystem of tools built on a shared foundation. The tools range from fully procedural generation — where the entire scene fills automatically from rules — to semi-procedural workflows where you guide placement with brushes, to manual level design tools that give you per-object control. All of them share the same data system, filter stack, and transform pipeline.
