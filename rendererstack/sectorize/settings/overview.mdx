Sectorize settings are split across three tabs:

- **Sectors** — generated sector scenes and scene-control actions.
- **Scene Settings** — shared scene components, including Cameras.
- **Global Settings** — Sectorize Streaming Rules.

## Included settings

- [Sectors](/rendererstack/sectorize/settings/sectors)
- [Streaming Rules](/rendererstack/sectorize/settings/streaming-rules)
- [Cameras](/rendererstack/scene-settings/cameras)
- [Scene Manager integration](/rendererstack/sectorize/scene-manager-integration)

## Streaming Rules

Streaming Rules control when sectors load, unload, or stay cached.

  <Card title="Asynchronous Loading" href="/rendererstack/sectorize/streaming-rules/asynchronous-loading">
